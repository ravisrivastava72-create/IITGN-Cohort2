## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 3, option a. Giving the losing team its own target beside the rate feels generous, which makes
it the tempting answer. A growth target catches no harm: it is one more number to chase. The one harm orders billed could show, a shrinking base, is
already on the page twice, in the count printed beside the rate and in the line on what the page does
not reward. What the son needs is to see growth, and the printed count already shows it.
